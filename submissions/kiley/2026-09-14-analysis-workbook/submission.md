assignment: Beta Forecast

I built out a full forecast for the PA-07 House race using real voter data. Along the way I checked which towns are easiest/hardest to call, found the town that best mirrors the whole district, and tried three different ways to forecast 2026; a simple trend, a "beta" method borrowed from finance, and a regression model. I then tested all three against real 2024 results to see which actually works. Spoiler: they don't fully agree with each other, so the last sheet lays out where they land and why that matters.
